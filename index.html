SafeContact

“Your Safety, Always Within Reach!”

SafeContact is an emergency safety platform designed for academic demonstration and personal preparedness. Developed as a prototype to demonstrate emergency contact access, consent-based location sharing, and safety information.

Share Live Location?

Consent required for emergency transmission

SafeContact requests permission to access and stream your GPS location coordinates. This allows your emergency contacts to see your precise location on a live interactive map during this alert.

  • Location is shared only with your authorized contacts.
  • You can tap "STOP LIVE LOCATION" at any moment to immediately halt sharing.
  • No location data is permanently saved or tracked without authorization.
